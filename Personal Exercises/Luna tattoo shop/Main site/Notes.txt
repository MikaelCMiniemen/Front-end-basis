OVERALL VISUAL STYLE

Direction:
Dark, moody, premium, slightly mystical. Light text on deep, complex dark backgrounds. Metallic accents used like jewelry: thin borders, small labels, dividers, buttons, progress bars, selected states. Not glittery, not neon, not pure black, not pure white.

HOW TO USE METALLIC ACCENTS

Thin 1px borders around cards, buttons, and input fields.

Divider lines between sections.

Small all-caps labels: "ARTIST," "DEPOSIT," "PAYMENT PLAN."

Selected calendar date, active tab, progress bar.

Hover state on buttons: subtle metallic sheen.

Logo or monogram: foil-stamp effect.

Icons: thin line style with metallic stroke.

Do not use metallic colors for long body text. They are accents only. Body text should stay light and readable.

TYPOGRAPHY

Headings: elegant serif or art-nouveau-inspired display. Think Cormorant, Cinzel, Marcellus, Recoleta, Ogg.

Body/UI: clean sans. Inter, Manrope, Satoshi, Space Grotesk.

Script font: only for logo or very short accent phrases.

Small labels: all caps with wide letter spacing.

Keep contrast high: light text on dark background, no low-contrast gray-on-gray.

TEXTURE AND MOTION

Subtle noise/grain on dark backgrounds.

Soft radial vignette.

Slow shimmer on metallic elements.

Ink-bleed or fade transitions.

Respect reduced-motion settings.

ACCESSIBILITY

Body text should meet at least 4.5:1 contrast.

Metallic accents often fail contrast. Use them for borders, icons, large text, or decorative states.

Focus states should be clearly visible: light outline or metallic ring.

Avoid relying on color alone for errors or success.

APPOINTMENT PLANNER NOTES

Goal:
Make booking feel calm, clear, and premium. Not like a generic booking form.

Flow:

Choose service: custom tattoo, flash, cover-up, consultation, touch-up.

Choose artist or "first available."

Pick date/time from real availability.

Enter details: placement, size, color/blackwork, references, budget, allergies, skin conditions.

Pay deposit or choose payment plan.

Confirmation with reminders and aftercare info.

Useful features:

Real-time availability.

Waitlist for fully booked artists.

Deposit required to lock the slot.

Clear cancellation/reschedule policy before payment.

SMS/email reminders.

Add to calendar.

Intake and consent forms before appointment.

Consultation vs. actual tattoo session distinction.

Mobile-first layout.

UI style:

Step progress indicator at top.

Sticky summary: artist, date, service, deposit, total estimate.

Calendar with muted unavailable dates and metallic selected date.

Forms with dark fields, light labels, metallic focus ring.

Buttons: dark fill, metallic border, light text.

Keep copy reassuring: "A deposit reserves your date and goes toward your final price."

Theming:

Use moon phases subtly for waitlist or availability, but do not make it confusing.

Artist cards: dark cards, metallic border, portrait, specialty tags.

LOG IN NOTES

Goal:
Low friction for clients, stronger security for artists/admin.

Client login:

Email magic link or Google/Apple sign-in.

Optional password.

Guest booking allowed, then prompt to create account after.

Account area: upcoming/past appointments, deposit receipts, aftercare, payment plan status, saved references, loyalty/referrals.

Artist/admin login:

Email + password + 2FA.

Session timeout.

Role-based access: artist, admin, front desk.

Dashboard for schedule, client notes, approvals, blocked time.

UX details:

Minimal login card on dark background with subtle metallic border.

Clear password reset.

Clear error states: use muted garnet or burnt sienna instead of bright red.

"Remember me" optional.

Terms and privacy consent visible.

Security notes:

Encrypt sensitive data.

Do not store card details directly.

Use trusted payment provider.

Log out inactive sessions.

PAYMENT / PAYMENT PLANS NOTES

Goal:
Make money feel transparent and flexible, not pushy.

Payment types:

Deposit to book.

Full payment.

Pay-per-session for large pieces.

Milestone payments: outline, shading, color, final session.

Gift cards.

Tips.

Aftercare products.

Payment plans:

Split into 2–4 installments.

Autopay with reminders.

Clear due dates.

Late fee policy stated upfront.

Deposit is usually non-refundable.

For large custom work: monthly plan or pay at each session.

UI elements:

Breakdown: estimated total, deposit, remaining balance, next payment.

Progress bar for payment plan — metallic fill.

"Pay now," "Manage plan," "View receipts."

Show cancellation/no-show policy before checkout.

Success state: muted patina green, not bright green.

Error state: muted brick or burnt sienna, not bright red.

Trust and security:

PCI-compliant provider: Stripe, Square, PayPal, Klarna, Afterpay, Affirm.

3D Secure where available.

Clear refund policy.

Email receipts automatically.

Do not store raw card numbers.

Theming:

Payment cards: dark background, metallic border, light text.

Buttons: "Reserve with deposit," "Choose payment plan," "Pay remaining balance."

Keep language simple: "You'll pay $X today. The rest is due at your session."
