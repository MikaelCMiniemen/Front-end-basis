About Luna

Meet the Artist

Hi, I'm Luna. I'm a fine-line and blackwork tattoo artist based in Leuven. I believe that tattoos are a way to reclaim your body and tell your own story. My style is heavily inspired by the moon, nature, and all things beautifully strange.

Whether you're getting your first tattoo or adding to a collection, I aim to make the process as comfortable, safe, and collaborative as possible. Every piece is custom-designed to flow with your anatomy and reflect your unique personality.

My Specialties

Fine-Line

Delicate, intricate designs with a focus on precision, elegance, and soft shading.

Blackwork

Bold, high-contrast pieces using deep black ink, geometric shapes, and heavy textures.

Custom Designs

Unique, one-of-a-kind designs created entirely around your ideas and stories.

The Process

  3. 3. The Session

Frequently Asked Questions

Does getting a tattoo hurt?

Yes, it can be uncomfortable, but it is highly manageable. Most clients find it feels like a constant scratching sensation. We take breaks whenever you need.

Do you do cover-ups?

Yes! Cover-ups are a great way to transform old tattoos. Please send a clear, well-lit photo of the existing tattoo when you book a consultation.

Are your inks vegan and cruelty-free?

Absolutely. I only use high-quality, vegan-friendly inks and single-use, sterile needles for every session.
