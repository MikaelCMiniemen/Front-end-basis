Home

The home page showcases recent work in a slideshow. The header features our logo and a navigation bar. Visitors can easily book an appointment, explore our services, or get in touch.

Appointments

Book a session with Luna. Select a service, choose a date, and complete the intake form with details like placement, size, style, and reference photos. A deposit secures your spot and goes toward the final price. Walk-ins are welcome if space is available.

Login

Clients can log in to view upcoming appointments, past sessions, saved references, and payment plan status. You also have the option to book as a guest and create an account later.

Contact

Have questions about a custom piece, cover-up, or consultation? Reach out via email or Instagram DM. The studio is based in Leuven and works by appointment only. Response time is typically within 48 hours.
